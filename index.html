Support tools for online talks

A background that names your talk and where to find you, and a way to turn the recording into stills worth posting. Everything runs on your machine; nothing is uploaded.

Backdrop

Meeting background with title, name, links and a QR code. Light or dark, one accent colour. Exports 1920×1080 PNG.

Picker

Drop a folder of stills, see them ranked by sharpness, crop to Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook or X, export.

Frames CLI

Cut stills from a recording between a start and end time, every N seconds or on scene changes. Uses ffmpeg.
