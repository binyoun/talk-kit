Make stills from a recording first:

Frames are scored in your browser for sharpness and for a face with open eyes and a closed mouth. Your images never leave this machine; the face model (~4 MB) is fetched once from Google's MediaPipe storage.

Pick a frame from the sheet. Use ← → to step through them.
